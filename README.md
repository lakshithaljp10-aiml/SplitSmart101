# 💸 SplitSmart – AI-Powered Bill Splitter

**SplitSmart** is an AI-powered expense-sharing application designed to make splitting bills among friends simple, accurate, and hassle-free.

Whether it's a restaurant outing, a trip, or a group activity, SplitSmart eliminates the confusion of manual calculations by using AI, automation, and intelligent expense management to handle everything from scanning bills to settling debts.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 👥 User & Group Management
- Create groups and add friends.
- Maintain separate expense records for each group.
- Manage individual contributions and shared expenses.

### 2. 📸 AI Bill Scanner
- Upload or capture restaurant bills.
- OCR automatically extracts item names, quantities, and prices.
- Converts scanned bills into structured expense data.

### 3. 🧠 Smart Item Allocation
- Uses NLP to understand natural-language instructions.
- Automatically identifies who ordered which items.
- Assigns individual and shared items to group members.

### 4. 🧮 Intelligent Bill Splitting
- Equal splitting.
- Unequal splitting.
- Item-wise splitting.
- Proportional splitting.
- Automatically accounts for taxes, discounts, service charges, and shared items.

### 5. 🔄 Debt Simplification
- Calculates how much each person owes.
- Determines who needs to pay whom.
- Minimises the number of transactions required to settle group expenses.

### 6. 📊 Expense Tracking
- Track individual contributions.
- Monitor pending and completed payments.
- Maintain expense history for every group.
- View settled and unsettled balances.

### 7. 🤖 AI-Based Expense Analysis
- Analyse individual and group spending patterns.
- Generate intelligent expense insights.
- Identify spending trends.
- Predict estimated future expenses using Machine Learning.

### 8. 🔔 Notifications & Reminders
- Notify members about newly added bills.
- Send reminders for pending payments.
- Notify users when payments are settled.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Create a Group:** Add friends who participated in an outing.
2. **Upload a Bill:** Capture or upload a restaurant receipt.
3. **Extract Information:** OCR extracts the items, quantities, and prices.
4. **Allocate Items:** Use simple text instructions to assign items to individuals.
5. **Split the Bill:** Choose an appropriate splitting method.
6. **Simplify Debts:** Automatically calculate the minimum transactions required.
7. **Track Expenses:** Monitor payments, contributions, and outstanding balances.
8. **Get Insights:** Analyse spending habits and receive expense predictions.

## 🧠 AI/ML Integration

SplitSmart incorporates intelligent technologies to reduce manual effort:

- **OCR:** Extracts structured information from uploaded bills.
- **NLP:** Understands natural-language item allocation instructions.
- **Machine Learning:** Analyses historical expenses and predicts future spending.
- **Algorithmic Optimisation:** Simplifies debts by minimising settlement transactions.

## 🎯 Project Objective

To develop an intelligent expense-sharing platform that automates bill scanning, item allocation, expense calculation, and debt settlement while providing meaningful insights into spending habits.
